Meet the team.

The DemCon × The Parlor’s new event series Democracy Salon launches September 20th at 6pm CET. Salon 1 will feature DemCon Founder Hunter Christopher, The Parlor Founder Lindsey Brock Morales, Design & Marketing lead Elizabeth Gallagher, and Head of Operations Louise A. If you want to learn about how this team is building the “CPAC of the Left” this is a must attend event.
